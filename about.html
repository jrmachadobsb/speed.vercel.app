About NetSpeed

NetSpeed is a free internet speed test tool that lets anyone check their connection's download speed, upload speed, ping, and jitter — instantly, without creating an account or installing anything.

Our tool uses Cloudflare's global speed measurement infrastructure to deliver accurate results. By connecting to servers close to your location, we measure real network performance rather than approximations.

How the test works

When you click "Start test", our tool runs three sequential measurements:

Ping: We send multiple small requests to a Cloudflare server and measure the round-trip time in milliseconds. We also calculate jitter — the variance between those measurements.

Download: We simultaneously download multiple large data chunks from Cloudflare's network and calculate your transfer rate in Megabits per second (Mbps).

Upload: We send data to Cloudflare's servers using XMLHttpRequest, measuring how quickly your connection can transfer data outbound.

Why we built this

Many speed test tools are bloated, slow to load, or require you to create an account. We wanted a fast, clean, and transparent alternative that anyone can use on any device. No trackers beyond what's disclosed in our privacy policy, no pop-ups, no dark patterns.

Accuracy and limitations

Results reflect real network conditions at the moment of the test. They can vary based on time of day, server load, your router's hardware, and whether you're testing over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. For best accuracy, close other apps and run the test multiple times at different hours.

Upload results in particular may differ from other speed test services due to CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) browser restrictions. We use XMLHttpRequest to measure upload progress as data is sent, which gives a real measurement of your outbound transfer rate.
