Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

NetSpeed ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website and use our internet speed test tool.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information automatically when you use our service:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, operating system, and referring URL.
  • IP address: Used to estimate your geographic region for routing speed test traffic. We do not store IP addresses linked to identifiable individuals.
  • Speed test data: Test results (download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter) may be collected in aggregate, anonymous form to improve the service.
  • Cookies: We may use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes as described below.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the speed test tool
  • To analyze and improve website performance and user experience
  • To measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns (via Google Ads)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Google Ads and advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google Ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting google.com/settings/ads or aboutads.info.

We do not control the content of Google Ads or the data Google collects for advertising purposes. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymized data about page views, session duration, and traffic sources. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for:

  • Analytics (understanding how the site is used)
  • Advertising (serving and measuring Google Ads)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features. Most browsers allow you to refuse, delete, or be notified about cookies.

6. Third-party services

Our speed test uses Cloudflare's network infrastructure (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure connection speed. When you run a test, your device communicates directly with Cloudflare's servers. Please review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this data.

7. Data retention

We do not store personally identifiable information. Aggregated, anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 26 months as permitted by our analytics provider's policies.

8. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
